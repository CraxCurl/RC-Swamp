RC UFO Drone Controls & Functionality Guide

System Architecture & Protocol

The RC UFO Ground Control Station connects directly to the drone's on-board Access Point over high-speed UDP (Port 7099) and decodes live camera RTSP streams (Port 7070) in real time.

🛸
GL & Legacy Protocol Engine

Automatically negotiates 21-byte GL or 9-byte Legacy packet formats with XOR checksums running at 25 Hz (40ms).

📡
Zero-Latency FPV Video

Decodes Motion-JPEG / H.264 streams from rtsp://192.168.1.1:7070/webcam with hardware accelerated PyAV demuxing.

🖼️
Image Processing Pipeline

Extract live frames directly via REST API or run python process_frames.py for OpenCV / AI processing.

⚖️
Sensor Trimming & Calibration

Fine-tune Roll, Pitch, and Yaw center drifts with hardware-level offset multipliers and 6-axis gyro leveling.

Dual Stick Channel Mapping

Stick / Axis Movement Keyboard Effect on Drone
Left Stick (Y-Axis) Up / Down W / S Throttle / Altitude: Increases/decreases motor RPM to ascend or descend.
Left Stick (X-Axis) Left / Right A / D Yaw / Rudder: Rotates drone clockwise or counter-clockwise on its vertical axis.
Right Stick (Y-Axis) Up / Down ↑ / ↓ Pitch / Elevator: Tilts drone forward (fly forward) or backward (fly backward).
Right Stick (X-Axis) Left / Right ← / → Roll / Aileron: Tilts drone sideways to bank left or bank right.

All Control Buttons & Functionality

🛫 TAKEOFF
One-Key Auto Takeoff: Sends the FastFly flag continuously for 1000ms. The drone spins all 4 motors, lifts off the ground, and stabilizes in hover at ~1.2 meters. Hotkey: SPACE.
🛬 LAND
One-Key Auto Landing: Sends the FastDrop flag for 1000ms. The drone smoothly descends until ground contact is detected, then disarms motors safely. Hotkey: SHIFT.
🔄 360° FLIP
Aerobatic Stunt Flip: Opens the stunt direction menu (Left, Right, Front, Back). Pulses the selected axis to maximum displacement with the CircleTurnEnd bit for 600ms to perform a tight 360° flip. Hotkey: F.
⚖️ CALIBRATE GYRO
6-Axis Gyroscope Calibration: Sends GyroCorrection bit for 2000ms while holding sticks at neutral center (128). Calibrates horizontal IMU level. Drone LEDs will flash rapidly during calibration. Hotkey: G.
🛑 EMERGENCY STOP
Emergency Motor Cutoff: Immediately sets EmergencyStop flag and cuts power to all 4 motors. Use only to prevent a crash or runaway situation. Hotkey: ESC.

Flight Modes & Trim Tuning

⚓ Altitude Hold (Fixed Height)

When enabled (default), the drone uses on-board optical flow and barometric pressure sensors to maintain current altitude automatically when the throttle stick is centered.

🧭 Headless Mode (Orientation Lock)

When active, the drone's forward direction is locked to the heading it had at takeoff. Pushing Pitch forward will always move the drone away from you, regardless of which way the drone's camera is facing.

⚡ Speed Gear Sensitivity Rates
  • 30% (Slow / Indoor): Stick range scaled to ±40 from center. Perfect for beginners and indoor precision flight.
  • 60% (Medium / Standard): Stick range scaled to ±60 from center. Responsive outdoor cruising with good wind resistance.
  • 100% (Fast / Pro): Full ±127 stick deflection authority (1..255). Maximum speed and aggressive agility.
◀▶ Trims (Roll, Pitch, Yaw)

If the drone drifts when sticks are centered, click the respective Trim buttons (◀ / ▶ / ▲ / ▼) to apply hardware offset adjustments to cancel drift.

Camera, Images & Vision Processing

  • 🖼️ Images Studio: Click the [🖼️ IMAGES / DATA] button to open the capture studio, view stored frames, and toggle continuous disk auto-saving.
  • 📷 Lens Switch ([C]): Switches between Front Lens and Bottom Optical Flow Lens via UDP packet [0x06, 0x01] / [0x06, 0x02].
  • 🐍 Python CV Pipeline: Run python process_frames.py to pull live frames for OpenCV / PyTorch / YOLO / NumPy processing.
  • 📸 Quick Save ([P]): Instantly captures and saves the live frame to ./camera_data/.

Drone Wi-Fi Pairing Guide

  1. Power on the drone. The camera gimbal will initialize and drone LEDs will flash.
  2. On your laptop/PC, open Wi-Fi settings and search for networks.
  3. Connect to the drone's open Wi-Fi network (SSID name starts with KY-WiFi-, WiFi-, 4K-WiFi-, or RC-UFO-).
  4. No internet is required. Keep the connection active.
  5. The Ground Station connects automatically to 192.168.1.1:7099 and displays live camera feed from rtsp://192.168.1.1:7070/webcam.
