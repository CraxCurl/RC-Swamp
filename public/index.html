CONNECTIVITY

Select Drone Connection Architecture

Choose how you want this Ground Control Station to communicate with the drone. You can switch modes at any time from the top header.

1. Direct Wi-Fi Connection

Laptop → Drone Wi-Fi Hotspot (192.168.1.1 / 192.168.4.1)
Your laptop is connected directly to the drone's Wi-Fi network (e.g. WIFI-UFO-xxxx, KY-WiFi-xxxx). Sends UDP flight packets and streams live RTSP video.
UDP 7099 / 8090 RTSP 7070 Video Full Video & Telemetry

2. ESP32 Bluetooth Bridge

Laptop → Bluetooth SPP → ESP32 → Drone Wi-Fi
Laptop communicates over Bluetooth to the ESP32_Drone. The ESP32 is connected to the drone's Wi-Fi and bridges all flight commands with zero latency.
